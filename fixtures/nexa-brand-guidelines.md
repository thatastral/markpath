# Nexa brand guidelines — version 2.0

## Colour

Primary blue: `#2457F5`

## Typography

Primary typeface: Geist

## Interface controls

Button radius: 8px

## Voice

Brand voice: Clear, direct and calm. Plain words, sentence case, no hype, no exclamation marks, no jargon.

## Accessibility

Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.

## Logo

Use the approved Nexa wordmark only. Do not use the legacy logo.

These rules supersede the previous primary blue `#164B9B`, Inter typeface, and 4px button radius.
